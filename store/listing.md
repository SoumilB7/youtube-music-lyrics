# Store listing

Copy these into the **Store listing** tab of the Chrome Web Store developer dashboard.

## Product details

**Name** (comes from `manifest.json`)
Lyricly — Synced Lyrics for YouTube Music

**Summary** (comes from `manifest.json`, 132 characters max)
Large, time-synced lyrics for YouTube Music, with Hindi lyrics automatically romanised into Hinglish.

**Category:** Entertainment
**Language:** English

**Description**

```
Lyricly puts large, time-synced lyrics in YouTube Music's Lyrics tab, and makes Hindi songs readable for everyone who doesn't read Devanagari.

HINDI IN HINGLISH
Hindi lyrics are romanised automatically, right in your browser: तू मेरा कोई ना होके भी कुछ लागे becomes "Tu mera koi na hoke bhi kuch laage". It follows how Hinglish is actually written, dropping the silent "a" sounds (धड़कन → dhadkan, not dhadakana). Show Hinglish, the original script, or both.

SYNCED TO THE SONG
The current line is highlighted and kept in view as the song plays. Click any line to jump to that part of the song. For songs with no timed lyrics, Lyricly spreads the lines across the song so the highlight stays close, and marks the timing as approximate.

FIX THE TIMING ONCE
If the lyrics run early or late (a music video with a longer intro, say), nudge them with − and +. Fixes made at different points stretch the lyrics to fit. Tap the bookmark to save the fix, and Lyricly applies it every time that song plays.

MADE TO READ
• Large text with adjustable size
• Full-screen mode with your album art behind the lyrics
• Lives inside YouTube Music's Lyrics tab, so Up next and Related stay where they are
• Opens the Lyrics tab automatically (you can turn this off)
• Alt+L shows or hides the lyrics

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. Romanisation happens on your device. Lyricly only runs on music.youtube.com.

CREDITS
Lyrics come from LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free, open-source lyrics database built by its community. If Lyricly helps you, consider contributing lyrics there.

Lyricly is open source: github.com/SoumilB7/youtube-music-lyrics

Lyricly is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
```
